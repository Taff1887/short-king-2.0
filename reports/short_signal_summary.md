# Short-signal quality summary

Per-model, per-bucket short-position outcomes. Every position is a single (Date, Ticker) short with realised one-month forward return; no costs, no portfolio construction.

**Columns**:
* `win %` = share of positions where the stock fell.
* `median` / `mean` = central tendency of the per-position short return (positive = stock fell, the short won).
* `mean win` / `mean loss` = average outcome conditional on winning / losing.
* `win/loss ratio` = `mean win / |mean loss|`. >1 = average win is bigger than average loss; <1 = losses dominate.
* `worst` = single worst position (the big squeeze).
